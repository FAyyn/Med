\section{Methodology}
We present CaMedPO, a causality-aware preference optimization framework for aligning medical vision–language models with clinically grounded evidence. CaMedPO reduces hallucinations by centering optimization on lesion-grounded visual cues and suppressing shortcut correlations that arise from modality misalignment.

The framework operates in three steps: (1) estimate the causal contribution of lesion evidence to candidate responses; (2) curate preference pairs that contrast lesion-grounded answers with context-biased alternatives under controlled foreground/background views; and (3) fine-tune using a weighted DPO objective that prioritizes clinically significant, causally reliable pairs. By integrating causal signals into sample weighting, CaMedPO preserves beneficial lesion–background interactions while attenuating spurious associations, improving robustness and factuality in Med-LVLMs.

\subsection{Clinical-Aware Preference Fine-tuning}
We integrate causal metrics into training by assigning clinical-aware weights and optimizing a weighted DPO objective with mDPO-style conditioning.

\subsubsection{Causality-Aware DPO Weighting}
\label{sec:weight_mapping}
We assign a weight $w$ to each preference pair to steer optimization. The weight derives from a composite score $S_{\text{raw}}$ that synthesizes causal metrics:
\begin{equation}
S_{\text{raw}} = (\Delta^+ - \Delta^-) + \alpha m_v - \beta \max(0, m_n - \tau_n) + \eta \, \delta.
\end{equation}
This score rewards the net TIE ($\gamma$) and correctness ($m_v$), penalizes background bias ($m_n$), and can include background-context gain $\delta$ when clinically meaningful. We normalize and map to a probability-like value $p \in (0,1)$ via a temperature-controlled sigmoid:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
S' &= \frac{S_{\text{raw}} - \mu}{\sigma}, \\
p &= \sigma(\beta_T S') = \frac{1}{1+e^{-\beta_T S'}},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where $\mu$ and $\sigma$ are the mean and standard deviation of $S_{\text{raw}}$, and $\beta_T$ is a temperature. The final training weight uses power transform with clipping for stability:
\begin{equation}
w = \operatorname{Clip}(p^{\kappa}, w_{\min}, w_{\max}).
\end{equation}
When $\Delta^- - \Delta^+$ exceeds $\tau_{\text{flip}}$, the foreground strongly supports an incorrect answer. In these anti-preference cases, we either flip the preference pair or apply a penalty factor $\rho$ to correct severe grounding failures.

\subsubsection{Weighted Objective (mDPO/DPO-style)}
Leveraging clinical-aware weights, we fine-tune the policy using a weighted DPO objective. Let $\pi_\theta$ be the policy and $\pi_0$ the reference. For a pair $(x, I, y^{+}, y^{-}, w)$, define the log-likelihood ratio contrasts
\begin{align}
\Delta_\theta &= \log \pi_\theta(y^{+}\mid x, I) - \log \pi_\theta(y^{-}\mid x, I),\\
\Delta_0 &= \log \pi_0(y^{+}\mid x, I) - \log \pi_0(y^{-}\mid x, I).
\end{align}
The weighted objective is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{wdpo}} = - \mathbb{E}\Big[ w \cdot \log \sigma\big( \beta (\Delta_\theta - \Delta_0)\big) \Big].
\end{equation}
We keep a unified weighted DPO objective with mDPO-style conditioning described in prose, without introducing additional per-pair loss equations, aligning with MMedPO's compact presentation.
